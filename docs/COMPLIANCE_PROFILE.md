# Ethics Compliance Portal
## Compliance Profile

**Version:** 1.0 (Working Draft)

---

# Purpose

The Compliance Profile is the central record created for every person reviewed by the Ethics Compliance Portal.

Every Portal output is generated from the Compliance Profile.

---

# Person

The Compliance Profile represents one individual.

Examples

- John Smith
- Jane Doe

---

# Reporting Year

Every Compliance Profile is created for one reporting year.

Example

2027

---

# Filing Bases

One person may have multiple filing bases.

Examples include:

- Elected Official
- Candidate
- Appointed Official
- Public Employee

Every filing basis must be evaluated independently.

---

# Offices

The Compliance Profile stores all offices relevant to the reporting year.

Examples

- Governor
- County Council
- Mayor
- School Board Trustee

---

# Campaigns

Every campaign is evaluated separately.

A person may have multiple campaigns.

Each campaign maintains its own reporting obligations.

---

# Required Filings

The Rules Engine determines every filing required for the reporting year.

Examples include:

- Annual Statement of Economic Interests
- Initial Campaign Report
- Quarterly Campaign Reports
- Pre-Election Campaign Report

---

# Filing Results

Each required filing receives one objective status.

Possible statuses include:

- Filed
- Filed Late
- Missing
- Manual Review Required

---

# Compliance Summary

The Compliance Profile summarizes every objective compliance determination for the person.

A person may have:

- multiple missing SEIs
- multiple campaign deficiencies
- both campaign and SEI deficiencies

The Compliance Profile combines all objective deficiencies into a single review.

---

# Outputs

Every Portal output is generated from the Compliance Profile.

These outputs include:

- Work Queue
- Excel Export
- Deficiency Letter
- Dashboard Statistics

---

# Human Review

The Compliance Profile does not determine:

- just cause
- legal defenses
- enforcement decisions

Those determinations remain the responsibility of Commission staff.
