# Ethics Compliance Portal
## Compliance Rules Library

**Version:** 1.0 (Working Draft)

---

# Purpose

The Compliance Rules Library documents the objective business rules used by the Ethics Compliance Portal.

The Portal automates objective compliance determinations. It does not replace the legal judgment of Commission staff.

---

# Core Principles

## Principle 1 – Reporting Year

All compliance reviews are performed for a selected reporting year.

The reporting year is not necessarily the election year.

---

## Principle 2 – Filing Basis

Every filing obligation must be based upon one or more filing bases.

Examples include:

- Elected Official
- Candidate
- Appointed Official
- Public Employee

A person may have more than one filing basis during the same reporting year.

---

## Principle 3 – Candidate Status

Candidates in partisan elections are required to file Statements of Economic Interests.

A person may simultaneously be:

- an elected official, and
- a candidate for another office or reelection.

The Portal must evaluate every filing basis independently.

---

## Principle 4 – Campaigns

Each campaign is evaluated separately.

Running for Governor and Attorney General at the same time represents two separate campaigns.

Campaign reporting obligations are determined independently for each campaign.

---

## Principle 5 – Compliance Review

The Portal determines:

- required filings
- actual filings
- timeliness
- objective deficiencies

The Portal does not determine legal defenses or enforcement outcomes.

---

## Principle 6 – Human Judgment

Commission staff determine:

- just cause
- legal defenses
- enforcement decisions
- investigative findings

The Portal only identifies objective compliance issues.

---

## Principle 7 – Person-Centered Review

The person is the unit of review.

All filing obligations belonging to the same person are evaluated together.

The Portal produces one Compliance Profile per person.

---

## Principle 8 – Multiple Deficiencies

A person may have multiple deficiencies.

Examples include:

- multiple missing SEIs
- multiple campaign reporting deficiencies
- both campaign and SEI deficiencies

All objective deficiencies are combined into a single Compliance Profile.

---

## Principle 9 – Work Queue

The primary operational output of the Portal is a person-centered work queue.

Each row represents one person's Compliance Profile.

---

## Principle 10 – Deficiency Letter

The primary correspondence generated by the Portal is a person-specific deficiency letter.

The letter summarizes all objective deficiencies identified during the compliance review.

Commission staff review the letter before it is sent.

---

# Living Document

This Rules Library is intended to evolve as statutes change, Commission procedures change, and the Portal gains additional capabilities.
